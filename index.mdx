# Understand what drives growth

Trfyx brings your website and product analytics into one place, so you can move beyond page views and investigate how visitors discover, experience, and convert on your product.

Track acquisition, explore user behavior, measure conversion, connect revenue to the journeys that drive it, and share the metrics that matter with your team.

## A practical path to value

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Trfyx">
    Add your site to Trfyx, follow the [Install tracking guide](/install-tracking) for the correct snippet and framework-specific steps, and confirm that events are arriving before you rely on reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the outcomes that matter">
    Configure goals around meaningful actions such as sign-ups, trial starts, activation, or purchases. Use names and definitions your team can consistently understand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investigate behavior">
    Use user journeys, session replay, and funnels together to understand the paths people take and where friction appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect acquisition to outcomes">
    Review attribution and revenue data to understand which sources and campaigns contribute to valuable actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the picture">
    Build and embed dashboards to give teammates a consistent view of the metrics they need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do with Trfyx

- **Product and website analytics:** Understand traffic and engagement across your digital product.
- **Session replay:** Inspect recorded sessions to add behavioral context to aggregate metrics.
- **User journeys:** Explore the paths visitors take through your site or product.
- **Funnels and goals:** Measure conversion through important flows and monitor key actions.
- **Revenue attribution:** Connect acquisition context to revenue outcomes where your integrations and tracking support it.
- **Data imports:** Bring supported analytics data into Trfyx for broader analysis.
- **Embedded dashboards:** Share analytics views with teammates or display them in other parts of your workflow.

## Before you begin

You’ll need access to your Trfyx workspace and permission to make the relevant changes to your website or application. Depending on your setup, installing tracking may require access to your site code, tag manager, or deployment workflow.

> **Important:** Reports depend on what you have installed, configured, and sent to Trfyx. Historical data is not guaranteed to appear automatically, and attribution quality depends on consistent tracking and correctly connected sources.

## Need help?

Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart). If something does not look right, verify your installation and event collection before interpreting the resulting reports.
