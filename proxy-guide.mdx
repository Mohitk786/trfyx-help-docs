A proxy routes Trfyx tracking requests through a domain you control instead of sending them directly to `app.trfyx.com`.

This can improve collection reliability when browser extensions, network filters, or ad blockers block known analytics domains.

<Note>
  A proxy is optional. Install the standard Trfyx tracker first, then add a proxy when you are ready for production traffic or want a first-party collection path.
</Note>

## Choose your setup

| Option | Best for | Code | DNS | Maintenance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Managed Proxy** | Most websites | None | Yes | Trfyx |
| **Self-hosted proxy** | Teams that already control a server, framework, or edge layer | Yes | Usually no | You |

### Managed Proxy

Use Managed Proxy when you want the simplest setup. You pick a subdomain, add the two DNS records shown in Settings, and click Check status. Trfyx issues HTTPS after the proxy is Active. The tracking snippet then loads from your subdomain, and events go to `/api/collect` on that same host.

<Tip>
  Managed Proxy is recommended for most sites.
</Tip>

## What should be proxied?

A working proxy needs to forward both:

1. The Trfyx browser script (`script.js`).
2. Event collection (`/api/collect`).

On Managed Proxy, loading the script from your subdomain is enough. The script posts to `/api/collect` on the same host. You do not set `data-api-url`.

For self-hosted setups, preserve the visitor's original IP information when forwarding requests. If every visitor appears to come from your server or proxy location, the proxy is not forwarding the client IP correctly.

## Verify installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your production website">
    Load a live page on your site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the network panel">
    Open **Developer Tools → Network** and reload the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the script loads through your domain">
    Check that `script.js` is requested from your domain rather than `app.trfyx.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger and confirm an event">
    Fire a pageview or tracked event and confirm the request also goes through your proxy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Trfyx for the activity">
    Verify the new visit or event appears in your Trfyx dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not use a proxy just because you can. A broken proxy is worse than no proxy: it can silently drop events or replace every visitor's IP with the server's IP. Verify collection and visitor location after deployment.
</Warning>
