Dodo Payments supports one-time and subscription payments. Connecting it to Trfyx lets you attribute revenue and track subscription lifecycles.

## What you'll need

- A Dodo Payments account with a business set up
- Your **Business ID** (from your Dodo dashboard)
- An **API key**
- The Trfyx tracker installed on your website

## Step-by-step setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Business ID and API key">
    Log in to your Dodo Payments dashboard. Your Business ID is shown in your account settings (starts with `biz_`).

    Create an API key if you don't have one already.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect in Trfyx">
    Go to **Site Settings → Payments** in your Trfyx dashboard.

    Select **Dodo Payments**, paste your Business ID and API Key, then click **Connect**.

    Trfyx creates a webhook that listens for:
    - `payment.completed` — successful one-time payments
    - `payment.refunded` — refunds
    - `subscription.active` — subscription payments
    - `subscription.cancelled` — cancellations
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Make a test payment. Verify it shows up in **Revenue → Overview**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Dodo data flows into Trfyx

| Dodo event | What Trfyx records |
|---|---|
| Payment completed | Payment + cascade attribution + customer identity |
| Payment refunded | Refund recorded, original payment flagged |
| Subscription active | Subscription payment + attribution |
| Subscription cancelled | Cancellation recorded |

## Disconnecting

Go to **Site Settings → Payments** and click **Disconnect**.
