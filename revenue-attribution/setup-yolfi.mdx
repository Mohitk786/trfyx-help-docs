Yolfi handles payments and subscriptions for online businesses. Connecting it to Trfyx lets you track which channels drive paying customers.

## What you'll need

- A Yolfi account
- Your **Merchant ID** (from your Yolfi dashboard, starts with `mer_`)
- An **API key**
- The Trfyx tracker installed on your website

## Step-by-step setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Merchant ID and API key">
    Log in to your Yolfi dashboard. Find your Merchant ID in account settings and create or copy an API key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect in Trfyx">
    Go to **Site Settings → Payments** in your Trfyx dashboard.

    Select **Yolfi**, paste your Merchant ID and API Key, then click **Connect**.

    Trfyx creates a webhook that listens for:
    - `payment.succeeded` — successful payments
    - `payment.refunded` — refunds
    - `subscription.created` — new subscriptions
    - `subscription.canceled` — cancellations
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Make a test payment and verify it appears in **Revenue → Overview**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Yolfi data flows into Trfyx

| Yolfi event | What Trfyx records |
|---|---|
| Payment succeeded | Payment + cascade attribution + customer identity |
| Payment refunded | Refund recorded, original payment flagged |
| Subscription created | New subscription record |
| Subscription canceled | Cancellation recorded |

## Disconnecting

Go to **Site Settings → Payments** and click **Disconnect**.
