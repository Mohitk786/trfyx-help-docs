Polar is a monetization platform for developers and open-source projects. Connecting it to Trfyx lets you see which channels drive your sponsorships, product purchases, and subscriptions.

## What you'll need

- A Polar account with an organization
- Your **Organization ID** (the "Identifier" in your org settings)
- An **Access Token** with the right scopes
- The Trfyx tracker installed on your website

## Step-by-step setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your Organization ID">
    Go to your [Polar Dashboard](https://polar.sh/dashboard) → **Settings → General → Profile**.

    Look for the field labeled **Identifier**. This is your Organization ID. Copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an access token">
    Go to **Settings → General → Developer → Token**.

    Create a new token with **no expiration** and select only these scopes:
    - `checkouts:read`
    - `orders:read`
    - `organizations:read`
    - `products:read`
    - `subscriptions:read`
    - `webhooks:write`

    Copy the token (starts with `polar_oat_`).

    <Tip>
    Keep permissions minimal. Trfyx only needs read access to your data and write access to create the webhook.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect in Trfyx">
    In your Trfyx dashboard, go to **Site Settings → Payments**.

    Select **Polar**, paste your Organization ID and Access Token, then click **Connect**.

    Trfyx creates a webhook that listens for:
    - `order.created` — purchases
    - `order.refunded` — refunds
    - `subscription.created` — new subscriptions
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify with a test transaction">
    Make a test purchase. Check **Revenue → Overview** for the payment and confirm the attribution looks correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Polar data flows into Trfyx

| Polar event | What Trfyx records |
|---|---|
| Order created | Payment + cascade attribution + customer identity |
| Order refunded | Refund recorded, original payment marked |
| Subscription created | New subscription record |

## Disconnecting

Go to **Site Settings → Payments** and click **Disconnect**.
